# General Store Guide

**Audience:** GMs and content authors. **Purpose:** describe the practical equipment catalogue and merchant variants.

The General Store provides 144 canonical products across Containers, Lighting & Fire, Rope & Climbing, Camping, Writing, Household, Animal Supplies, Travel, Tools and Trade Goods. Shared products retain the same IDs and base values in other merchant catalogues.

## Products

Descriptions define the complete sale unit, inclusions and manual-use limits. Native containers have empty weight and explicit capacities; goods sold filled account for contents/packaging once. Mundane utility actions do not create unlisted combat, healing or skill benefits. Light metadata is reference information, not token-light automation.

Prices intentionally make copper and silver useful. Adjusted weights are stated per product; ordinary supplies still have transport significance. See [Price Guide](PRICE_GUIDE.md) and [Weight Guide](WEIGHT_GUIDE.md).

## Merchant variants

Village, Town, City and Merchant Wagon share one catalogue. Their profiles alter core/rotating pools and exclusions through data rather than copying products. The Wagon excludes bulky stock. Settlement/prosperity further scale Merchant Builder generation and cash.

Build Items and Stock RollTables, apply an editable preset to an NPC, save, then inspect stock and cash previews. Generated stock preserves existing product identities. There is no automatic restock schedule. See [Builder Guide](BUILDER_GUIDE.md).
